# Comparus banking assistant

- IBM source: [Comparus case study](https://www.ibm.com/case-studies/comparus-gmbh)
- Hub section: Resources
- Reviewed: 2026-09-28

## Page notes

- The case study describes using process mining to choose a banking workflow, then combining conversational interaction with calls across multiple business systems.
- IBM reports improvements for that customer and describes further integration and regulatory work. The reported numbers are case-specific and should not be generalized to Laravel Guild.
- The transferable pattern is to measure a process before automating it and keep integration and oversight in view.

## Check against Laravel Guild

- Capture a baseline delivery before claiming an agent upgrade improves throughput or cost.
- Keep tool calls bounded by the existing [security governance](../../agent-security-governance.md) and [evaluation engineering](../../evaluation-engineering.md) contracts.

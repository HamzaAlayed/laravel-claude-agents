# Riyadh Air: AI native operations

- IBM source: [Riyadh Air case study](https://www.ibm.com/case-studies/riyadh-air)
- Hub section: Resources
- Reviewed: 2026-09-28

## Page notes

- IBM presents the airline's operating model as a coordinated system of data, workflows, and specialized agents rather than isolated automations.
- The case study describes a large integration effort across partners and applications. A central orchestration layer routes tasks while human teams retain oversight for work that needs judgment.
- Its main lesson for this pack is architectural: useful agents need reliable interfaces, shared context, and operational control around them.

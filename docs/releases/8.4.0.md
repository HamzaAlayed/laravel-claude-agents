# Laravel Guild 8.4.0

## What’s new

Interrupted stage claims now have an explicit recovery lifecycle. A resumed
delivery can distinguish a live claim from a stranded one, preserve observed
work, and continue through a fresh atomic claim.

- `guild recovery list` shows active claims and durable recovery events.
- `guild recovery interrupt` freezes a confirmed interruption with a typed
  source, exact reason, and stable event ID.
- `guild recovery resolve --action continue` requeues without consuming the
  stage retry; `--action stop` fails the stage and delivery.
- `ready` carries the recovery context into the resumed specialist brief.
- Generated `recoveries.md` and `transitions.md` files preserve the audit trail.

See [Recover interrupted claims](../recovery-policy.md) for the operating
procedure and command examples.

## Breaking changes

There is no change for completed or normally running deliveries. Automation
that previously treated every persisted `running` claim as safe to reuse must
now inspect `recovery list`. After confirming that the old runtime is gone, it
must record and resolve the interruption before claiming the lane again.

Direct edits to `kernel.json`, reused reports, and fabricated completion
telemetry remain unsupported and are now explicitly blocked by the recovery
contract.

## Fixes

- A process exit can no longer strand a stage in `running` with no supported
  route back to `queued`.
- Resuming no longer charges unobserved offline time or invents turns, tokens,
  and cost for a missing completion receipt.
- Interruption does not consume the one failure retry or reset cumulative known
  usage.
- An interrupted delivery remains protected by native-write and Bash policies
  instead of falling through to the direct single-specialist fast path.
- Known elapsed activity that reached the stage time limit still ends as
  `budget_exceeded`; recovery cannot bypass a hard budget.

## Compatibility

Existing delivery state loads with empty recovery history. Legacy active
claims use their claim timestamp as the last known activity and do not gain
synthetic events. Direct point work outside a planned delivery is unchanged.

## Verification

- Focused recovery tests cover the state machine, accounting, authority,
  idempotency, budget precedence, generated views, compatibility, and CLI.
- Full kernel tests cover interactions with retries, checkpoints, budgets,
  pairing, verification, and workplace feedback.
- Guardrail tests bind all nine pipeline commands and the delivery coordinator
  to the recovery lifecycle and keep interrupted deliveries fail-closed.
- The console, eval, manifest, generator, shell, frontend, and hosted CI gates
  pass before publication.
